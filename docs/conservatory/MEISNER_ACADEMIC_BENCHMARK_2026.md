# SLAMS Meisner curriculum — evidence-backed institutional benchmark
**Research checked:** 2026-11-02. **Status:** research specification, not an affiliated/official school syllabus or curriculum release.

## Rule 1: Which Meisner school is being mirrored?

There is **no single exact, shared "major Meisner course syllabus."** Different institutions publish differing program length, hours, supporting subjects, faculty evaluation and teaching lineage. Their public pages do NOT disclose a reproducible week-by-week class and drill schedule. SLAMS cannot claim exact lesson equivalence, private handout equivalence, credit transfer or institutional endorsement without documented institutional authorization and access to the relevant materials.

### Primary structural comparator — Neighborhood Playhouse (published facts)
First party: https://www.neighborhoodplayhouse.org/two-year-conservatory
Program: **two academic years; 32 weeks per year; approx. 30–32 hours/week full-time**. First-year **Meisner Acting Technique I: 12 hours/week**; second-year **Meisner Acting Technique II: 12 hours/week**. Second year is **by invitation** following faculty evaluation. The official first-year core includes repetition, improvisation, independent activity and scene work; the second year applies advanced improvisation, interpretation and character development to demanding dramatic texts. Source accessed 2026-11-02.

**First-year published subject families:** Meisner Acting Technique I; Acting Lab (Drills); Theatre History I; Modern Dance I; Music I / Singing Technique I; Jazz Dance I; Shakespeare I; Voice I and Speech I; Alexander Technique I; Physical Theatre I; Stage Combat I. Published hours vary by class. These are **official listed course families**, not evidence that every listed class runs concurrently or that SLAMS delivers its hours. Movement/voice work involving physical supervision requires appropriately qualified instruction.

**Second-year published subject families:** Meisner Acting Technique II; Shakespeare II; Acting for Film & Television (including voiceover and auditioning topics); Alexander Technique II; Jazz Dance II; Theatre History II; Modern Dance II; Music II / Acting on Pitch; Physical Theatre II; Professional Development; Voice II and Speech II. There are productions and public performances. These are **not** replaceable by an automated online quiz.

Published source for invitation: https://www.neighborhoodplayhouse.org/faq-1 — faculty and the Executive Director consider attendance, motivation, participation, preparedness and growth for Year 2; criteria are **not** a published numerical pass mark. A SLAMS assessment must not pretend to award their invitation.

### Independent comparator — William Esper Studio
First party: https://esperstudio.com/two-year-program/
Two-year training with Acting Technique I (first year **6 hours/week**) and Acting Technique II (second year **6 hours/week**) plus supplementary voice, movement, improvisation, scripts, on-camera and professional training depending on track. The second year builds advanced character and interpretation. This differs materially from Neighborhood Playhouse's twelve-hour weekly technique. The public FAQ https://esperstudio.com/faq/ says actors trained in Meisner elsewhere are **generally encouraged to begin first year**, because lineages differ. SLAMS advanced placement is therefore our own proposed educational mechanism, not their transfer policy.

### Separate academic model — NYU Tisch Meisner Studio
Source: https://tisch.nyu.edu/drama/about/studios/the-meisner-studio/curriculum
Has a first-year actor-instrument foundation, second-year transformation/character work and a further advanced production-focused year. Do **not** mix its third year into a claimed official Playhouse or Esper syllabus.

## Rule 3: Two layers of instructional truth

Every school/term/exercise carries:
- **Institutional benchmark:** school name, source URL, publicly confirmed class families/duration, claim scope, last checked, rights/authorization status.
- **Independent SLAMS pathway:** independently authored term/year subdivisions, named exercises, lesson text, application, accessibility, evidence contract and assessment policy. All such content is labeled SLAMS-designed, reviewed or draft.

Without a licensed/school-authorized internal syllabus, use the label **"SLAMS Meisner training pathway — independently designed from published Meisner teaching principles"**. Do not say **"exact Neighborhood Playhouse curriculum"**, **"Esper-certified"**, **"study the same official program"**, or any exact week-by-week school equivalence claim.

## Rule 4: Learning architecture and complete exercise contract

Immutable route: School → Year → Term → Course → Workshop → Lesson → Exercise → Attempt → Evidence → Assessor review → Eligibility → Advancement. Preserve prerequisites and parallel voice/movement/text work rather than reducing the conservatory to repetition.

An exercise cannot be tagged instructional-release-ready unless each field is substantive and method-specific:

1. WHY (observable goal and educational rationale)
2. THEORY / LINEAGE / verified claim source, properly scoped
3. WHAT (objective and conditions)
4. HOW (numbered instructions, setup and a real demonstration)
5. WHEN to use
6. WHAT NOT TO DO, including unsafe/misapplied practice
7. SOLO mode: valid independent exercise, preparatory only, or not suitable; explain equivalence limits
8. PARTNER mode: genuine human-partner procedure and consent
9. GROUP mode: authentic ensemble variation or reason not applicable
10. COMMON PITFALLS (specific, not reused filler)
11. CORRECTION / failure recovery
12. PROFESSIONAL APPLICATION (audition, stage, film or rehearsal)
13. PRACTICE TARGET (official source-backed quota, faculty-calibrated SLAMS quota or approved competence protocol; otherwise NULL)
14. EVIDENCE / REVIEW (what proves participation vs competence, rubric and evaluator)
15. ACCESSIBILITY / CONSENT / DATA RIGHTS
16. PREREQUISITES / NEXT STEP / content version and faculty approval

No fabricated official repetition counts, sample thresholds, institution-issued awards or emotional-truth scores.

## Rule 5: Progress and qualified advancement (Content 04 contract)

Track separately: **opened**, **understanding demonstrated**, **solo prepared**, **partner practiced (self-reported)**, **evidence submitted**, **under instructor review**, **faculty-accepted / needs revision / not assessable**, **SLAMS milestone earned**.

Attempts and quiz success are **NOT** mastery. Actor-submitted evidence cannot self-assign faculty approval. Count only accepted, properly scoped evidence under a published policy and suitable modality. For partner-required Meisner tasks, solo sessions are prep only. In-progress work must survive interruption and identity switching without leaking to other actors.

Every unlock must be **server-authoritative**: under a database transaction check the currently published lesson/term, prerequisites, policy version, distinct accepted evidence, qualified instructor signature, and the exact school + actor. Protect with RLS; no browser-supplied "pass", timestamp or reviewer identity is sufficient. Preserve audit events, appeals and revocations.

## Rule 6: Advanced actor placement — challenge, not a shortcut button

On each school / year / term overview, offer **"Request Advanced Placement"**. No bypass on declaring "professional". A learner may browse open/public descriptions, but **cannot receive assessed credit or skip gates without a review decision**.

Proposed SLAMS placement steps:
1. Select school and exact entry target (workshop, term or year) and view the competencies they must demonstrate.
2. Declare relevant prior training (optional; self-description alone awards nothing).
3. Complete versioned knowledge/application tasks specific to the target's **prerequisite graph**.
4. Supply consented evidence of genuinely required modes (live partner demonstration for partner Meisner; flexible accessible alternatives may be reviewed).
5. Apply a concrete instructor note and retake a fresh/changed task.
6. Supply an evidence-grounded reflection about observable changes.
7. Qualified assessor checks distinct prerequisites against a **faculty-approved placement rubric**; otherwise state NEEDS_REVIEW.
8. Server signs an exact actor-school-target-version decision. Grant **only the specific approved SLAMS access**, never blanket official conservatory year credit. Failed/inconclusive challenges yield focused preparatory work and fair reassessment/appeal.

**No numerical passing score exists in original SLAMS Content 03/04 yet.** Store NULL until qualified Meisner faculty validate a rubric and policy. Any "80% to unlock Year 2" rule would be invented and invalid. Esper's generally-start-with-Year-1 policy is further evidence against universally easy transfer.

## Rule 7: Honest student-facing screens

School Page: Official-public program benchmark (cited) next to explicitly separate SLAMS track map, reviewed content availability, parallel disciplines, current standing, and Request Placement.

Exercise Page tabs: **WHY / HOW / WHEN / AVOID / SOLO / PARTNER / GROUP / PITFALLS / APPLY / PRACTICE & EVIDENCE**. Tabs must not exist empty: require release-ready content or an honest preview state.

Practice Tracker: dated attempts, solo/partner mode, actor's objective/reflection, observable change, optional permitted media and participation-only partner attestations, evidence status, assessor notes, actionable correction, next eligible step. No percentage based merely on taps or a timer.

Placement Page: purpose and prerequisite domains, accessible demonstration requirements, consent controls, submitted evidence, review status, targeted revision, authorized unlock. When backend/instructor queue absent, label **NOT YET AVAILABLE** and do not imply that submitting a form awards access.
