## 4. Two different progression systems — NEVER conflate them

### Non-academic guided workshop navigation (implemented)
1. Read three complete lessons.
2. Answer three authored concept-classification questions correctly.
3. Select solo preparation or partner mode; partner mode requires voluntary agreement and stopping/recording boundary declarations.
4. Record **three complete, nonidentical, learner-authored rounds** for each of three original exercises. The widget blocks proceeding to the next exercise until the three drafts are logged.
5. Write a reflection and specific next adjustment.
6. Copy an optional private note explicitly; data is not written to browser storage, Passport or Supabase.

**What this proves:** The app received nonempty written answers and the knowledge-check selections were correct. **What this does not prove:** An actor performed correctly, gave valid analysis, obtained independently verified partner consent, or met academic standards.

### Academic assessment and legitimate advancement (NOT activated)
- Authenticated learner submits evidence attached to a specifically reviewed/published curriculum content version.
- Qualified, actively authorized faculty reviewer observes actual work and records rubric-specific evidence, including partner responsiveness where required.
- University-style grade/pass threshold must be explicitly approved; **currently null**, not a guess.
- Approved progression requires the existing server-controlled academic policies and RLS, not frontend state, self-declared experience, copied notes, or an LLM grade.
- An experienced actor may request **advanced placement** only through the same assessed faculty-authorized mechanism; no jump is granted from a self-report or simple quiz.
- Term completion/graduation follows independent reviewed requirements across performance, text analysis, voice/movement, ensemble and research as actually applicable to the selected curriculum.

## 5. Draft human-performance rubric (not a score)

The app now shows five observable domains:
1. Consistency with given circumstances; fact vs interpretation.
2. Attention to an actual task and a changed physical action.
3. Specific objective expressed through a playable tactic.
4. Responsive change when an obstacle arises.
5. Listening to a consenting human partner; never credited through solo writing alone.

Each criterion describes evidence and an insufficient response; grading weights, pass requirements, appropriate accommodations and faculty appointments are deliberately **unset** until professional review.
